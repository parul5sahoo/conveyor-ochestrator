# Belt Mechanical Integrity (Level 2 Specialist Discipline)

**Parent Domain**: `conveyor-diagnostics`

Calibrates ultrasonic belt tension and tracks splice degradation across conveyor rollers.

## Operational Micro-Skills (Level 3)
- **[`tension-ultrasonic-calibration`](./micro-skills/tension-ultrasonic-calibration/SKILL.md)**: Measures belt resonant vibration frequency to calibrate take-up carriage tension without stopping line.
- **[`splice-wear-tracking`](./micro-skills/splice-wear-tracking/SKILL.md)**: Inspects mechanical vulcanized splice seams via high-speed line scan cameras to prevent catastrophic belt parting.
