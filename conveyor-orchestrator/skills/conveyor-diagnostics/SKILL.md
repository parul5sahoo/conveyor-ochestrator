# Conveyor Health & Motor Diagnostics (Level 1 Root Domain Skill)

3-tier conveyor predictive maintenance suite covering motor vibration analysis, VFD thermal monitoring, ultrasonic belt tension, and splice wear.

## Specialist Disciplines (Level 2)
- **[`motor-drive-diagnostics`](./disciplines/motor-drive-diagnostics/SKILL.md)**: Analyzes motor vibration spectra, bearing fault harmonics, and variable frequency drive (VFD) thermal loads.
- **[`belt-mechanical-integrity`](./disciplines/belt-mechanical-integrity/SKILL.md)**: Calibrates ultrasonic belt tension and tracks splice degradation across conveyor rollers.
