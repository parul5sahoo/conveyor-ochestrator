# HR & Workforce Governance (Level 1 Root Domain Skill)

3-tier workforce policy orchestrator covering employee leave, shift differentials, overtime, ergonomics, and OSHA clinic triage.

## Specialist Disciplines (Level 2)
- **[`employee-leave-attendance`](./disciplines/employee-leave-attendance/SKILL.md)**: Administers paid time off, medical certification rules, FMLA entitlements, and shift scheduling compliance.
- **[`compensation-shift-differentials`](./disciplines/compensation-shift-differentials/SKILL.md)**: Calculates off-peak shift premiums, holiday multipliers, and hazardous zone hourly bonuses.
- **[`occupational-health-ergonomics`](./disciplines/occupational-health-ergonomics/SKILL.md)**: Audits repetitive strain risks, NIOSH lifting standards, anti-fatigue rotations, and on-site clinic triage.
