# Occupational Health & Ergonomics (Level 2 Specialist Discipline)

**Parent Domain**: `hr-workforce-governance`

Audits repetitive strain risks, NIOSH lifting standards, anti-fatigue rotations, and on-site clinic triage.

## Operational Micro-Skills (Level 3)
- **[`repetitive-strain-assessment`](./micro-skills/repetitive-strain-assessment/SKILL.md)**: Calculates Recommended Weight Limits (RWL) per NIOSH lifting equation (max 51 lbs unassisted) and enforces 2-hour workstation rotation.
- **[`clinic-incident-triage`](./micro-skills/clinic-incident-triage/SKILL.md)**: Guides emergency first-aid triage, automated nurse dispatch, and mandatory OSHA 300 log incident recording.
