# On-Site Clinic First Aid & OSHA 300 Triage (Level 3 Operational Micro-Skill)

**Hierarchy Path**: `hr-workforce-governance` ➔ `occupational-health-ergonomics` ➔ `clinic-incident-triage`

## Description
Guides emergency first-aid triage, automated nurse dispatch, and mandatory OSHA 300 log incident recording.

### Standard Operating Procedure: On-Site Clinic Incident Triage
1. **Immediate Aid**: Deploy on-site certified First Responder within 180 seconds to the reporting workstation.
2. **Severity Classification**: Minor First Aid (Bandage/Ice Pack) vs Recordable Medical Treatment (Stitches/Fracture/Concussion).
3. **OSHA 300 Recording**: Any incident resulting in lost workdays or restricted duty must be logged within 24 hours.
