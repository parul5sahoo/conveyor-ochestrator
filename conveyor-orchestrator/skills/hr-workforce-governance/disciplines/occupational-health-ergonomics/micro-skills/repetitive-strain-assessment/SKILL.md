# NIOSH Ergonomic Lifting & Rotation Assessment (Level 3 Operational Micro-Skill)

**Hierarchy Path**: `hr-workforce-governance` ➔ `occupational-health-ergonomics` ➔ `repetitive-strain-assessment`

## Description
Calculates Recommended Weight Limits (RWL) per NIOSH lifting equation (max 51 lbs unassisted) and enforces 2-hour workstation rotation.

### Standard Operating Procedure: NIOSH Lifting & Ergonomics
1. **Maximum Manual Lift**: The absolute single-person lifting limit is 51 lbs (23 kg) under ideal conditions.
2. **Two-Person Lift Mandate**: Any package exceeding 51 lbs requires a mechanical lift aid or team lift (2 operators).
3. **Workstation Rotation**: Sorters and decanters must rotate between packing, sorting, and palletizing every 120 minutes.
