# Employee Leave & Attendance Management (Level 2 Specialist Discipline)

**Parent Domain**: `hr-workforce-governance`

Administers paid time off, medical certification rules, FMLA entitlements, and shift scheduling compliance.

## Operational Micro-Skills (Level 3)
- **[`medical-certification-fmla-audit`](./micro-skills/medical-certification-fmla-audit/SKILL.md)**: Enforces the mandatory 3-consecutive-day sick note certification rule, continuous/intermittent FMLA eligibility (1,250 hours worked), and HIPAA-compliant physician note validation.
- **[`shift-swap-overtime-approval`](./micro-skills/shift-swap-overtime-approval/SKILL.md)**: Verifies 8-hour mandatory rest intervals between consecutive shifts and collective bargaining overtime fairness.
