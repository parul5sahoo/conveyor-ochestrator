# Medical Certification & FMLA Audit (Level 3 Operational Micro-Skill)

**Hierarchy Path**: `hr-workforce-governance` ➔ `employee-leave-attendance` ➔ `medical-certification-fmla-audit`

## Description
Enforces the mandatory 3-consecutive-day sick note certification rule, continuous/intermittent FMLA eligibility (1,250 hours worked), and HIPAA-compliant physician note validation.

### Standard Operating Procedure: Medical Certification & FMLA Audit
1. **Sick Leave Certification Mandate**:
   - Absences of 1 to 2 consecutive workdays do NOT require a physician note (self-certified via the warehouse attendance portal).
   - Absences of **3 or more consecutive calendar or scheduled workdays** strictly REQUIRE an official Medical Practitioner Certification prior to returning to active duty.
2. **FMLA Eligibility Verification**:
   - Employee must have completed at least 12 months of service and a minimum of 1,250 hours worked in the preceding 12-month period.
   - Provides up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per rolling calendar year.
3. **Return-to-Work Clearance**:
   - Employee must submit the clearance form to HR / Occupational Health Nurse 24 hours prior to shift restart.
   - Any physical lifting restrictions (e.g., maximum 20 lbs) must be transmitted to floor dispatch for light-duty zone assignment.
