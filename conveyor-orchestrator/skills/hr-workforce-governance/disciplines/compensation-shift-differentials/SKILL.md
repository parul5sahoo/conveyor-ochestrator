# Compensation & Shift Differentials (Level 2 Specialist Discipline)

**Parent Domain**: `hr-workforce-governance`

Calculates off-peak shift premiums, holiday multipliers, and hazardous zone hourly bonuses.

## Operational Micro-Skills (Level 3)
- **[`night-shift-differential-calc`](./micro-skills/night-shift-differential-calc/SKILL.md)**: Applies +15% evening shift (16:00-00:00) and +22% graveyard/night shift (00:00-08:00) differential calculations to base hourly wages.
- **[`holiday-premium-hazard-pay`](./micro-skills/holiday-premium-hazard-pay/SKILL.md)**: Applies 2.0x holiday multipliers and extreme temperature (cold chain freezer) hazard bonuses ($2.50/hr).
