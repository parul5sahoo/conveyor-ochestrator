# Holiday Premium & Hazard Pay Allocator (Level 3 Operational Micro-Skill)

**Hierarchy Path**: `hr-workforce-governance` ➔ `compensation-shift-differentials` ➔ `holiday-premium-hazard-pay`

## Description
Applies 2.0x holiday multipliers and extreme temperature (cold chain freezer) hazard bonuses ($2.50/hr).

### Standard Operating Procedure: Holiday & Hazard Premiums
1. **Holiday Premium**: Hours worked on recognized enterprise holidays are remunerated at 2.0x base pay.
2. **Cold Chain Freezer Hazard Pay**: Operators assigned to Sub-Zero Zone 4 (-20C) receive a flat +$2.50/hr hazard stipend.
3. **High-Rack Fall Hazard Pay**: Operators operating turret trucks above 30ft receive +$1.75/hr certified altitude stipend.
