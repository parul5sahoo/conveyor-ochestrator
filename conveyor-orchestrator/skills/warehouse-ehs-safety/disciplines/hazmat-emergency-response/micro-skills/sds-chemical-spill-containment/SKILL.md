# Safety Data Sheet (SDS) Spill Containment & Neutralizer (Level 3 Operational Micro-Skill)

**Hierarchy Path**: `warehouse-ehs-safety` ➔ `hazmat-emergency-response` ➔ `sds-chemical-spill-containment`

## Description
Identifies chemical containment actions, neutralizing absorbents, and required PPE level (Level A-D) from SDS CAS numbers.

### Standard Operating Procedure: SDS Chemical Spill Response
1. **Evacuate Upwind**: Clear minimum 50-foot radius upwind of liquid spill.
2. **PPE Selection**: Level B (chemical splash suit with SCBA) for toxic volatiles; Level C (nitrile gloves, apron, full-face respirator) for corrosive acids.
3. **Deploy Containment Sock**: Place polypropylene absorbent booms around floor drains to prevent sewer contamination.
