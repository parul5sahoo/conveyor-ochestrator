# Hazmat & Emergency Response (Level 2 Specialist Discipline)

**Parent Domain**: `warehouse-ehs-safety`

Directs chemical spill isolation, SDS neutralizer selection, and RFID muster point evacuation headcounts.

## Operational Micro-Skills (Level 3)
- **[`sds-chemical-spill-containment`](./micro-skills/sds-chemical-spill-containment/SKILL.md)**: Identifies chemical containment actions, neutralizing absorbents, and required PPE level (Level A-D) from SDS CAS numbers.
- **[`evacuation-headcount-audit`](./micro-skills/evacuation-headcount-audit/SKILL.md)**: Reconciles live badge RFID swipes at external assembly muster zones against active shift roster.
