# Energy Isolation & Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) (Level 2 Specialist Discipline)

**Parent Domain**: `warehouse-ehs-safety`

Standardizes electrical and pneumatic zero-energy physical verification and multi-technician padlock protocols.
